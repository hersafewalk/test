01 / About us

Safety should make people feel more empowered.

MyShieldLabs is an early-stage personal safety company. We are building with empathy, listening to everyday concerns and exploring ways to make personal safety feel more accessible and connected.

Our mission

Transform research insights into practical solutions that help women feel safer, more confident, and better connected. We aim to listen, understand and innovate around everyday safety needs.

Our vision

A world where a safe environment for women is a fundamental right, not a luxury — supported by thoughtful technology, stronger connections and people who can respond when help is needed.

03 / Our first product

Meet HerSafeWalk.

● Currently in development

HerSafeWalk is a personal safety wearable being developed with women’s everyday experiences in mind. It is being shaped around a simple idea: a discreet way to communicate a safety status and request help, with a companion phone experience.

✦A quick way to signal
Designed around a simple, easy-to-reach interaction.
✦Everyday check-ins
Concepts for communicating SAFE or UNSAFE status.
✦Discreet to carry
A wearable form designed to fit into daily life.
✦Clear feedback
Designed to make interactions feel understandable.

04 / The intended experience

Safety communication, made more approachable.

HerSafeWalk is being designed around a clear customer experience. The exact product experience will be confirmed as development and validation progress.

01 / WEAR

Carry it naturally

A discreet wearable concept intended to fit into everyday routines.

02 / CHECK IN

Share how you feel

Simple safety states designed to communicate when things feel okay or uncomfortable.

03 / SIGNAL

Request attention

An SOS interaction designed to help the wearer signal an urgent need for help.

04 / CONNECT

Bring in trusted people

A companion experience is being developed to support communication with selected contacts.

05 / Our approach

Thoughtful products start with people.

We are an early-stage team. Our priority is to understand the problem, shape a useful experience and build confidence through careful development.

01

Understand first

Listen to the people a safety product is meant to serve, and take their everyday concerns seriously.

02

Keep it simple

Make the experience clear, considerate and easy to understand — especially in stressful moments.

03

Earn trust over time

Develop responsibly, learn from feedback and communicate honestly about what is ready and what is not.
