03 / Product in development

Introducing HerSafeWalk.

● Prototype development

HerSafeWalk is a wearable personal safety pendant being developed to connect with an Android phone over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). Its intended experience centres on quick safety status updates, an SOS interaction and clear feedback from the device.

✦One-touch SOS concept
Designed to send an emergency event to the companion phone.
✦SAFE / UNSAFE status
Simple signals to communicate how the wearer is feeling.
✦LED + vibration feedback
Visual and haptic confirmation for interactions.
✦Motion sensing research
IMU-based motion features are planned for later development.

04 / Concept experience

A simple interaction flow. A connected response.

The intended HerSafeWalk experience uses a wearable pendant and a paired Android phone. Actual workflows will be validated during development and testing.

STEP 01

Pair & wear

Connect the pendant to the companion Android app over Bluetooth Low Energy.

STEP 02

Share SAFE status

Use the SAFE button to communicate that everything is okay.

STEP 03

Signal UNSAFE

Send a discreet status event when a situation feels uncomfortable.

STEP 04

Trigger SOS

Use the SOS control to send an emergency event to the paired phone.

05 / Technology

Engineered at the intersection of hardware and software.

HerSafeWalk’s current V1 design direction combines a compact embedded system with phone-based connectivity and emergency communication.

MicrocontrollerESP32-C3
Wireless linkBluetooth Low Energy
Motion sensorMPU6500 · 6-axis IMU
User controlsSAFE · UNSAFE · SOS
FeedbackRGB LED + vibration
Power concept3.7V rechargeable Li-Po
Development note: These are design targets and selected components from the current product plan, not claims of a finished, certified or commercially released device. Battery life, enclosure dimensions, water resistance, reliability and regulatory compliance require validation through testing.
